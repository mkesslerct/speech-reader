Good morning, everyone.
It is a pleasure for the Universidad Politécnica de Cartagena to welcome you to the II International Symposium on Green and Sustainable Chemistry 2026.
I will not be very original if I say that we are living in a time of major challenges — energy, sustainability, technology, climate, resources. I am not saying that the challenges we face today are greater than those faced by previous generations. Every generation has had its own difficulties. But it is clear that we, as a society, must find our way through all these changes and challenges.
And if there is one thing we should be clear about, it is that we will not do this without science.
It is true that some of the greatest challenges we face today are linked to technology itself. But it is equally true that, when guided by the right principles and values, science and technology are also key to the solutions — solutions that help us take better care of people, of resources, and of the environment.
Science is essential. Not as an abstract idea, but as a practical way to understand problems better and to find real solutions. Looking at the programme, it is very clear that the discussions you will have here over these days are precisely about that: solutions. Better technologies, more sustainable systems, more efficient ways of doing things.
So let me simply thank you for the work you do every day — in your labs, in your research groups, in your universities and institutions. Thank you for the solutions you help develop, for the progress you make possible, and for the knowledge you contribute to society.
And thank you for coming here to share ideas, discuss together, and continue building better solutions collaboratively.
I would also like to thank the organizers for making this symposium possible.
