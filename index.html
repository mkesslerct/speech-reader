Good morning, everyone.
I am Mathieu Kessler, Rector of the Universidad Politécnica de Cartagena.
Today we open the Mar Menor Living Lab.
The Mar Menor is Europe's largest saltwater lagoon. It is an extremely rich but fragile ecosystem. Pollution, nutrients, extreme weather — all leave their mark.
It calls for solutions, for the great sustainability challenges that it faces. And these solutions can also open opportunities for entrepreneurship.
It is therefore a perfect scenario to frame an international summer school for entrepreneurship.
Twenty-eight students. Four universities. Welcome to the students from New York University, to the students from TU Dublin, to the students from Cyprus University of Technology, and of course to the students of UPCT.
This school is a perfect illustration of our vision of what learning can mean in a technological university like ours.
This vision is based on the following principles:
There is no greater motivation to learn and get trained than searching for a solution to a problem. Even more, when it is not a personal problem, but a collective one. A problem whose solution has impact on a whole community. This is part of the approach here.
Diverse teams lead to better solutions. This week is interdisciplinary. You will work with people who bring different knowledge, different skills, different contexts. Internationalization is a powerful way to bring diversity, and therefore to learn. This is what EUT+ stands for — our alliance of universities, our European University of Technology.
Learn from the best. You will have the privilege to listen and interact with some of the best experts in their field. Passionate people, who are references in their field. It pushes us to go deeper in our effort, in our preparation. So that we can have real impact. Passion and training, combined.
Try to get the whole picture. The panelists have different experiences and approaches to the problem, administration, residents, scientists. Listening to their different viewpoints will oblige you to practice critical thinking.
Learning happens also outside of the classroom! You will work in the territory. Our environment is our teaching living lab.
For our students, entrepreneurship is a natural option. Engineering, technology — they exist to solve challenges. To search for solutions to problems.
